Brazilian Daily  Weather  Gridded  Data (BR-DWGD)
Description
This page presents basic information about the daily  meteorological gridded data set from Brazil
Variables
Precipitation (pr, mm); maximum and minimum temperature (Tmax, Tmin, °C);  solar radiation (Rs, MJ/m 2 ); relative humidity (RH, %); wind speed at 2 meters (u2, m/s) and evapotranspiration (ETo, mm).
Temporal resolution
Daily
Temporal coverage
1961/01/01-2025/12/31
Spatial resolution
0.1 ° x 0.1 °
Spatial coverage
Just Brazil territory
Latitude: 33.85 ° S - 5.35 ° N
Longitude: 73.85 ° W - 34 . 85° W
Methods
See paper

Realease notes
November 2026: Hourly Tmax , Tmin , Rs , RH and u2 grids are available for the period from 2007/01/01 to 2025/12/31 ( beta version available here ). The grids were generated using data exclusively from INMET automatic weather stations. For more information, here .
May 202 6 : update daily and monthly grid  "v_3.2.4" in Google Earth Engine, respectively here   and here .
April 2026: update grid (from 1961/01/01 to 2025/12/31, version "v_3.2.4" ). The report for this version is currently under construction and it is available here .
December 2024: update grid (from 1961 / 01 / 01 to 2024 / 03 / 20, version "v _ 3 . 2 . 3" ), with support from Climaterna project.
June 2023: we adopt a new boundary for the grid, with a better fit to the coast of North East;  corrections in the gain and offset of NetCDF data to open properly in the software QGIS (version "v_3.2. 2 ");
April 2023: all ncfiles were created with smaller size (version "v_3.2.1");
March 2023: update precipitation grid adding more rain gauges. Just for the  period 2001/01/01-2022/12/31 (version "v_3.1")
